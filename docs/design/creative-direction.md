# Creative Direction — An Experience Worth Visiting

**Working concept:** "From discovering the place to imagining your own celebration."

## Desired emotional progression
1. **Wonder:** immersive hero film introduces the real venue.
2. **Trust:** a confident, grounded explanation of the Recanto Miami experience.
3. **Discovery:** explore the spaces through horizontal parallax.
4. **Clarity:** discover services and practical differentiators.
5. **Proof:** browse real weddings and open their albums.
6. **Connection:** see and read authentic couples' stories.
7. **Action:** share event details and request a quote through WhatsApp.
8. **Closure:** final cinematic brand moment and direct contact.

## Design rules
- One coherent visual language from section to section, primarily inspired by Healing Minds.
- Treat transitions as narrative connectors; a zoom should have a reason and destination.
- Prioritize visual continuity, generous whitespace and a controlled color rhythm.
- Real media must carry the emotional weight; typography and motion support the images.
- Use complex effects sparingly: hero movement, spaces parallax, one 3D bridge, weddings infinite gallery are already sufficient high-impact moments.
- Follow established commercial inquiry behavior, not an experimental funnel.
- Provide graceful mobile and accessibility alternatives without sacrificing premium feeling.

## Key art-direction questions Claude should explore in Figma
- Does the hero video need a full-bleed title overlay or an editorial text panel?
- What type scale and framing best complements the actual existing film?
- How does the shift from ivory/daytime to dark green/evening support the wedding narrative?
- How will interactive components look *before, during and after* animation?
- What is the cleanest visual return from an opened album to the gallery?

## Copywriting
All published site copy should be written in **pt-BR** even though this documentation is in English. Existing site copy is the baseline; new headings and microcopy can be proposed but must be reviewed. Avoid unsupported factual claims (capacity, amenities, prices, guarantees, awards, inclusions) until verified from the venue.
