# Landing Page — Proposed Information Architecture

**Status:** Eight-section structure welcomed by the owner-side project team; titles, sequencing and exact content remain subject to design review. Do not turn it into implementation work before approval.

| # | Section (English working name) | User-facing Portuguese heading (proposal only) | Primary job | Interaction | Commercial path |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 01 | Cinematic Hero | Seu grande dia começa aqui | Create a strong first impression using the existing venue video | Autoplay muted film, subtle entry and video/image scale transition | Clear invitation to visit/request a quote |
| 02 | About Recanto Miami | Conheça o Recanto Miami | Express venue identity and tell its story | Editorial scroll storytelling and restrained image reveal | Optional contextual link |
| 03 | Our Spaces | Descubra nossos espaços | Show the actual physical environments | **Horizontal Parallax Gallery** | Light, nonintrusive CTA if helpful |
| 04 | Experiences & Services | Tudo para o seu momento especial | Explain verified offerings / event formats | Smooth, readable reveals | **WhatsApp inquiry entry point** |
| 05 | Real Weddings | Histórias que aconteceram aqui | Showcase five wedding groups, each with a full album | **3D transitional bridge** then **Infinite Scroll Gallery** | Albums become proof; keep navigation back |
| 06 | Testimonials | Histórias de quem viveu esse momento | Build trust from authentic couples and client messages | Editorial image + quote carousel | Contextual contact access |
| 07 | Plan Your Event | Vamos planejar seu evento? | Answer practical questions and gather intent | Calm, form-first UX; optional FAQs | **Same WhatsApp inquiry flow** as #04 |
| 08 | Final CTA & Footer | Vamos conversar? | Close with clarity and access | Cinematic but lightweight conclusion | WhatsApp, map/location, business contact |

## Important distinctions
- Section 03 explores **spaces**; section 05 explores **events**. Their galleries must not feel like redundant photo displays.
- Section 05's infinite behavior must not trap the user inside a permanent gallery; normal scroll progression and exit need to remain clear.
- Section 04 and 07 share the **same lead collection model**, but may introduce it with different context and CTA labels.
- Section 07 is not permission to invent a long planning questionnaire. Audit the existing inquiry flow first.
- The hero and final CTA should not compete with the two primary inquiry points.
- Use real existing services and event information from source materials. Mark anything not yet verified as a placeholder in prototypes.

## Navigation (proposal)
A lean, anchored navigation may feature "O Recanto", "Espaços", "Casamentos", "Depoimentos", "Contato" plus a persistent quote/visit CTA. Confirm exact labels during design approval.

## Responsive priorities
On small screens, keep message hierarchy and media impact while reducing animation complexity. Preserve both access to wedding albums and WhatsApp lead capture. Explicitly prototype mobile for every nonstandard scroll interaction.
