# Brand Visual Identity — Botanical Luxury

**Status:** Approved color direction from Stage 01. This document restates the existing palette, not a replacement identity.

## Positioning
A nature-rich wedding and event venue that feels warm, modern, refined, emotional and welcoming. The brand should communicate a memorable celebration in a real, beautiful setting — never an artificial, generic luxury aesthetic.

## Approved color palette

| Color | Hex | Intended use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Forest Green | `#263B31` | Primary brand surface, headings, elevated navigation and dark sections |
| Warm Ivory | `#F7F3EB` | Main light background, breathing space, readable content |
| Champagne Gold | `#C5A475` | Sparing highlights, accents, fine lines and detail |
| Natural Sand | `#E8DFD1` | Secondary background and gentle sectional changes |
| Deep Night Green | `#172B24` | Cinematic dark surfaces and overlays |
| Soft Graphite | `#252923` | Body text and neutral accents |
| Dusty Rose | `#D8C1BA` | Subtle emotional accent; use rarely |

Suggested visual distribution, **a flexible guide rather than a hard layout constraint**: approximately 60% light surfaces, 25% green and 15% secondary/accents. Champagne gold is a punctuation mark, not a full-panel color.

## Signature aesthetic
- Botanical refinement: natural textures, verdant spaces, warm light, understated ornament.
- Editorial luxury: striking typography, generous margins, careful photography crops.
- Day-to-night narrative: allow ivory / sand sections to transition into forest and deep-night green where the story supports it.
- Real footage and real event photography must be the emotional center.
- Motion should be quiet, smooth and intentional; not a carnival of visual effects.

## Typography (NOT YET APPROVED)
Claude should propose a sophisticated display serif with a clean companion sans-serif. Compare at least two commercially usable pairings with pt-BR accents; test actual headings, CTA legibility and mobile rendering. Do not treat a specific font as locked.

## Imagery rules
- Prefer authentic Recanto Miami photos/video over stock or generated substitutes.
- Human stories (couples, guests and testimonials) need privacy/usage checks.
- Avoid overpowering greens, gold gradients, generic wedding stock photography or artificial fake testimonials.
- Do not stretch, heavily distort or overprocess original photographs.

## Accessibility
Maintain readable contrast for text/CTA over imagery; never depend on a color change alone to convey a state. Text must remain legible with moving video behind it.
